# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

scrcpy-launcher is committed to maintaining a welcoming, safe, constructive,
and equitable open-source community.

Everyone who participates in the project is expected to treat others with
respect and to contribute in good faith. Participation must not be made hostile
or exclusionary on the basis of personal identity, background, experience,
ability, beliefs, nationality, socioeconomic status, or other personal
characteristics.

## Expected behavior

Healthy participation includes:

- engaging with others respectfully and honestly;
- keeping discussions focused on the project and the work at hand;
- recognizing that contributors may have different backgrounds, experience
  levels, communication styles, and viewpoints;
- giving and receiving constructive technical feedback without making it
  personal;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and correcting them when possible;
- crediting the work and ideas of others appropriately;
- respecting clearly expressed boundaries;
- helping maintain a useful, professional, and sustainable project community.

Disagreement is allowed and is often useful. Criticism of code, design,
documentation, decisions, or technical reasoning is not a violation of this
Code of Conduct when it remains focused on the work rather than the person.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted personal attention;
- insults, personal attacks, demeaning remarks, or deliberate humiliation;
- discrimination or derogatory stereotyping;
- sexualized conduct or unwelcome sexual attention;
- threats, encouragement of violence, or other conduct that endangers others;
- publishing or using another person's private information without permission;
- impersonating another person or using alternate identities to evade
  moderation;
- intentionally disrupting project discussions or repeatedly derailing issues,
  pull requests, or other collaboration;
- spam, unsolicited commercial promotion, or other material unrelated to the
  normal purposes of the project;
- presenting restricted, abusive, or harmful material irresponsibly;
- other behavior that would reasonably undermine the safety or health of the
  project community.

## Project spaces

This Code of Conduct applies to participation in spaces operated for
scrcpy-launcher, including:

- GitHub issues and pull requests;
- code review and commit discussions;
- GitHub Discussions or other project-hosted collaboration spaces, if enabled;
- project documentation and community-maintained content;
- official project websites or social accounts;
- communications made by someone acting as an official representative of the
  project.

Conduct outside project-controlled spaces may be considered when it directly
affects the safety of project participants or involves someone representing the
project in an official capacity.

## Reporting a problem

Possible Code of Conduct violations should be reported to the project
maintainer.

Because scrcpy-launcher is currently maintained by one person and does not
operate a dedicated private reporting system for conduct matters, do not place
sensitive personal details in a public GitHub issue. Instead, open a minimal
issue stating that you need a private contact channel for a Code of Conduct
matter. The maintainer will provide an appropriate way to continue the report
privately.

Reports should include only the information needed to understand the incident,
such as where it occurred, what happened, and any relevant messages or links.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith. The privacy and safety of reporters and
other affected people will be considered when deciding what information, if
any, should be made public.

Knowingly false or bad-faith reports may themselves be treated as a violation
of this Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement responsibility

The project maintainer is responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code
of Conduct in project-controlled spaces.

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove comments; close or lock issues
and pull requests; reject contributions; restrict participation; or take other
reasonable moderation actions when conduct does not meet these standards.

Moderation decisions should be based on the behavior and its effect on the
project community rather than on agreement or disagreement with a contributor's
technical position.

When practical and appropriate, the reason for a moderation action will be
communicated to the affected participant.

## Enforcement guidelines

Responses should be proportionate to the severity, persistence, and impact of
the conduct. The following progression is a guideline rather than a mandatory
sequence, and serious incidents may justify skipping earlier steps.

### 1. Warning

For a first or relatively minor violation, the participant may receive a
private or public warning explaining what behavior must change.

Where appropriate, repair may include acknowledging the problem, correcting a
statement or action, or offering an apology.

### 2. Temporary participation limits

For repeated behavior or a more serious first incident, participation may be
limited for a defined period or within particular project spaces.

The participant is expected to respect the restriction and avoid attempts to
continue the prohibited interaction through alternate channels.

### 3. Temporary suspension

For serious conduct or a pattern that continues after earlier intervention, the
participant may be temporarily blocked from project interaction.

Conditions for returning may be specified when appropriate.

### 4. Permanent ban

A participant may be permanently banned when severe conduct, repeated
violations, harassment, threats, deliberate evasion of moderation, or other
behavior makes continued participation incompatible with the safety or health
of the community.

Permanent bans are intended for cases where lesser measures are inadequate.

## Good-faith technical disagreement

This Code of Conduct is not intended to suppress technical criticism or require
agreement with maintainers or contributors.

Participants may challenge implementation choices, architecture, security
assumptions, documentation, project direction, or other technical decisions.
Such disagreement should remain evidence-based and focused on the work.

Maintainers may reject a contribution or technical proposal without that
decision being a conduct sanction. Likewise, a contributor may disagree with a
maintainer's technical decision without violating this Code of Conduct.

## Changes to this policy

Material changes to this Code of Conduct will be made publicly in the project
repository.
